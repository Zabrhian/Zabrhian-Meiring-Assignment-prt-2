# Tkayys Photography Website

## Project Overview

Tkayys Photography is a photography business website designed to showcase professional photography services and provide customers with an easy way to learn about the business, view its services and make enquiries.

The website provides information about Tkayys Photography, its photography services, contact details and service areas. It also includes enquiry and contact forms to make it easier for potential clients to communicate with the business.

The website was developed using HTML, CSS and JavaScript and is designed to provide a simple, professional and user-friendly experience across desktop, tablet and mobile devices.

## Website Goals and Objectives

The main goal of the Tkayys Photography website is to create a professional online presence for the business and make it easier for potential clients to learn about its photography services.

### Objectives

- Promote Tkayys Photography and its photography services.
- Showcase photography work and memorable moments through images.
- Provide clear information about the business and its services.
- Allow potential clients to make enquiries through the website.
- Provide contact information and service areas for clients.
- Provide clickable social media and communication links.
- Make the website easy to navigate on desktop, tablet and mobile devices.

## Website Features

The website includes the following features:

- Five connected HTML pages.
- Consistent navigation menu across all pages.
- Photography images and business content.
- Portrait, Event and Creative Photography service information.
- Enquiry form for potential clients.
- Contact form on the Contact Us page.
- Embedded Google Map showing the Summerstrand service area.
- Responsive CSS styling for different screen sizes.
- JavaScript form validation and submission feedback.
- Footer containing four columns of business information and links.
- Clickable Instagram, Facebook and WhatsApp links.
- Email and telephone contact links.
